The Pricer agent

Every time a Scout saves a recipe, this agent builds the Kroger shopping cart needed to cook it and works out its nutrition. Meal Planners then compare recipes by cost per serving and nutrition, without searching Kroger themselves.

Its instructions

This is the prompt the agent follows. It sets how many people the cart feeds, how to choose products, and when to skip an ingredient. A change applies to recipes priced from then on; use Price again for earlier ones.
