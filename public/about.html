About Meal Squad

Meal Squad was created by Toni Moreno for the purpose of learning: a hands-on exercise in building AI agents that work together as a system.

What it’s for

The exercise lets you learn by building. In a single session, groups build AI agents in Lovable and connect them into a system that plans five nutritious, varied and affordable dinners. Along the way you see how agents:

  • use tools and real data, rather than making things up;
  • follow rules they read from a shared server over MCP, instead of having them written into their code;
  • hand their work to other agents through a coordinator;
  • deal with rejections, surprises and failures, and explain them honestly.

The goal is understanding, not a perfect system. Some steps won’t work the first time, and that’s part of what there is to learn.
