Add the agent: a backend function run-planner that uses Lovable AI with tool calling, the coordinator's tools except save_meal_plan (saving comes in the next step), and finish(summary).

How it runs:
- It takes the budget and the "anything else" text from the page.
- Run the tools the model asks for and send back the results until it calls finish, or until 30 tool calls or 3 minutes (constants I can change). Record why it stopped.
- Save every step (thought, tool call, result, error) to a table agent_steps as it happens.

Instructions for the model (fill in BUDGET and NOTES from the page):
"You are Meal Planner. Plan five dinners, Monday to Friday, within a budget of BUDGET US dollars per person for the week. Also consider: NOTES. First call get_expectations and follow it, but don't save yet: once check_meal_plan shows your best plan, call finish with a short summary."

Connect the page: "Plan my week" starts a run, and "What the agent is doing" shows each step live. When the run finishes, "The week" shows the plan from the last check_meal_plan answer: for each day the photo, name, cost per serving, the agent's estimated calories and protein, and why; below the days, the week's cost per person against the budget and each rule with ✓ or ✗.
