Add the agent: a backend function run-planner that uses Lovable AI with tool calling, the coordinator's tools except save_meal_plan (saving comes in the next step), and finish(summary).

How it runs:
- It takes the budget, the dietary requirements and the preferences from the page.
- Run the tools the model asks for and send back the results until it calls finish, or until 30 tool calls or 3 minutes (constants I can change). Record why it stopped.
- Save every step (thought, tool call, result, error) to a table agent_steps as it happens.

Instructions for the model (fill in BUDGET, REQUIREMENTS and PREFERENCES from the page; write "none" for an empty box):
"You are Meal Planner. Plan five dinners, Monday to Friday, within a budget of BUDGET US dollars per person for the five dinners. Dietary requirements, which every dinner must meet: REQUIREMENTS. Preferences, to follow where you can: PREFERENCES.
Your goals: dinners that are nutritious and balanced, varied (different cuisines and main ingredients), and as cheap as possible within the budget. Use only priced recipes from the coordinator. For each dinner, estimate the nutrition of one serving from its ingredients and number of servings. When two choices are otherwise equally good, prefer the one more groups picked, then the cheaper one.
First call get_expectations to learn how to work with the coordinator, and follow its formats and checks. Check drafts with check_meal_plan and improve the plan until every check passes, but don't save yet: once check_meal_plan shows your best plan, call finish with a short summary that says the nutrition figures are estimates. If no plan can meet the requirements and the checks, say which can't be met and why. Never invent a recipe or a number."

Connect the page: "Plan my week" starts a run, and "What the agent is doing" shows each step live. When the run finishes, "The week" shows the plan from the last check_meal_plan answer: for each day the photo, name, cost per serving, the agent's estimated calories and protein, and why; below the days, the week's cost per person against the budget and each rule with ✓ or ✗.

Fill the outcome area:
- "Valid plan found" when the last check_meal_plan passed every rule.
- "No feasible plan found" when the agent finished without a plan that passes, with its explanation.
- "Run failed" when the run stopped on an error or a limit, with the reason.
