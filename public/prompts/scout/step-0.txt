Create a web page for an application called “Recipe Scout.”

Recipe Scout is one part of a class project in which several apps work together to plan a week of dinners. We are building only Recipe Scout. Its one job is to find recipes: it does not plan meals, choose days or calculate prices. Other apps do that.

Recipe Scout will eventually use AI to:

- Take a dinner theme from the user
- Search a real recipe database
- Find exactly four recipes that fit the theme
- Examine each recipe before selecting it
- Send the selected recipes to a coordinator, which passes them on to the other apps

For now, build only the interface. Do not add AI, a backend, a database or connections to other services yet.

Include:

- A welcoming title and a short explanation of what Recipe Scout will do
- A box labeled “What kind of dinners are you looking for?”
- These clickable examples:
  - Cheap vegetarian weeknight dinners
  - Comfort food for a rainy day
  - Dinners ready in about 20 minutes
- A “Scout recipes” button
- An empty area with space for four recipe cards, where the selected recipes will eventually appear

Nothing needs to happen when the button is pressed yet.

Make the page warm, inviting, responsive and accessible.
