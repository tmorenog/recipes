The Shopper Agent

Compares the meal plans the Meal Planner Agents saved, chooses the one the class will cook, and does the class’s shopping: the week’s Kroger cart for it.

About stock. Kroger only says roughly how much a store has (plenty, low or out of stock), not how many, and it can be out of date. So the Shopper Agent marks lines worth checking, Check stock, when Kroger says stock is low or out, or when the class needs 10 packages or more; for those it looks for a larger size first. Stock availability may be unreliable: double-check quantities in the store before buying for 50 people.
