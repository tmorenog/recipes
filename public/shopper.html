The Shopper Agent

Compares the meal plans the Meal Planner Agents saved, chooses the one the class will cook, and has the coordinator build its Kroger shopping cart for the week.

  1. The Meal Planner Agents save plansAll plans go to the coordinator.
  2. The Shopper Agent compares themChecks passed, cost, variety and popularity.
  3. The coordinator builds the cartThe plan is priced per person; the cart scales it to the number of people shopping, in whole packages.
  4. The class’s plan appears hereWith the reason it was chosen.

For the instructor: run the Shopper Agent once the Meal Planner Agents have saved their plans. It uses the AI key the Pricer Agent uses, and takes a minute or two.

Clearing removes the chosen plan, the earlier choices and the Shopper Agent’s past runs (the meal plans stay), e.g. before class starts.
