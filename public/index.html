Agents exercise

Welcome! Today your group works on an agent system.

You’ll build an AI agent in Lovable that does one job and saves its results to this site, the coordinator. Other agents pick up from there, each doing its own part. Start with the first agent and make sure you understand what it does; if you have time, keep going.

Your agentDoes one job with real data and tools
saves its results
The coordinatorThis site: a shared database that checks everything agents save and shows the class’s results
other agents build on them
More agentsEach reads what the others saved and adds its part

What you’ll do

  1. Get the class key and choose a group name

    Your instructor gives everyone the same class key. Your group picks a name, like team-3, which labels everything your agents save.

  2. Build your first agent, step by step

    Each step says what it adds and gives a sample prompt to paste into Lovable. Test after every step.

  3. Watch the results

    The Database page shows everything the class’s agents saved, and every attempt the coordinator turned down, with the reason.

  4. If there’s time, keep going

    The tabs at the top lead on to the next parts of the system. One agent you understand well is a good result.

Start building

The class key and your group name

Your agents send two things with everything they save:

  • The class key, the same for everyone, from your instructor. Treat it like a password: store it in Lovable as a secret when a prompt asks for it, and never paste it into a prompt, your code, or a chat. Only your app’s backend should use it.
  • Your group name, which your group chooses, e.g. team-3. Use the same name for every agent you build. Type it on the building pages and the prompts fill it in for you.

Good to know

Rejections are normal

The coordinator checks everything your agents save. When something’s wrong it says exactly what, e.g. “est_servings must be at least 1”. A good agent reads the reasons, fixes them, and tries again.

Everything is visible

Everything the class’s agents save, and every attempt, appears on the Database page. Use it to check your agent did what you think it did.

Build in small steps

Paste one step at a time and test before moving on. When something breaks, tell Lovable what you expected and what happened instead.
