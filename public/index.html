Agents exercise

Welcome! Today you build two agents that plan a week of dinners.

Your group builds two AI agents in Lovable. The first finds real recipes. The second turns recipes into five balanced, affordable dinners, priced at a real Kroger store. This site connects them: it holds the shared database, checks everything your agents send, and shows the whole class’s results as they happen.

What you’ll do

  1. Get the class key and choose a group name

    Your instructor gives everyone the same class key. Your group picks a name, like team-3, which labels everything your agents save.

  2. Build Agent 1, the Recipe Scout

    Follow the Recipe Scout page: what it must do, how to talk to the coordinator, and prompts to paste into Lovable.

  3. Build Agent 2, the Meal Planner

    Follow the Meal Planner page. It reads the recipes the Scouts saved, prices them at Kroger, and saves a plan.

  4. Watch the results

    The Database page shows every recipe, every meal plan, and every attempt your agents made, including the ones the coordinator turned down and why.

The two agents

Agent 1

Recipe Scout

Given a theme like “cheap weeknight vegetarian dinners”, it searches TheMealDB, reads full recipes, picks five that fit, and saves each one to the coordinator in the agreed format.

  • Uses tools: search, look up, save
  • Fixes and retries when a recipe is rejected

Build the Recipe Scout

Agent 2

Meal Planner

Reads the new recipes, prices every ingredient at a nearby Kroger, estimates nutrition, and picks five dinners that are balanced and within budget. Saves the plan with a shopping list, then marks the recipes as used.

  • Plans across many tool calls
  • Lets code do the maths, the model the judgment

Build the Meal Planner

The class key and your group name

Your agents send two things with everything they save:

  • The class key, the same for everyone, from your instructor. Treat it like a password: store it in Lovable as a secret when a prompt asks for it, and never paste it into a prompt, your code, or a chat. Only your app’s backend should use it.
  • Your group name, which your group chooses, e.g. team-3. Use the same name for both agents. Type it on the agent pages and the prompts fill it in for you.

Good to know

Rejections are normal

The coordinator checks every recipe and plan. When something’s wrong it says exactly what, e.g. “est_servings must be at least 1”. A good agent reads the reasons, fixes them, and tries again.

Everything is visible

All groups’ recipes, plans and attempts appear on the Database page. Use it to check your agent did what you think it did.

Build in small steps

Paste one prompt at a time and test before moving on. When something breaks, tell Lovable what you expected and what happened instead.
